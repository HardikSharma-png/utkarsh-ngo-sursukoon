Why We Exist

Our Mission

Utkarsh Samajik Seva Sansthan is dedicated to the holistic development of society through Dridh Sankalp (firm resolve), Nihswarth Seva (selfless service), and Jankalyan (people's welfare).

We work at the grassroots level to address the needs of marginalized communities — empowering women and youth, conserving water, promoting health, protecting the environment, and ensuring consumers have access to justice. Our belief is simple: a better society begins with informed, empowered, and healthy citizens.

What We Do

Core Mandate Areas

🏥

Health-Based Outreach

Disease prevention, family planning, and community health awareness programmes.

📢

Community Awareness

Nukkad natak, video screenings, and public campaigns to spread social awareness.

🤝

Social Development

Support for handicapped, widows, and elderly through targeted development programmes.

💻

Education & Computer Training

Digital literacy, management education, entrepreneurship, and pre-exam training camps.

🌿

Environment Protection

Jal Bachao Abhiyan and pollution prevention to protect our ecosystem.

⚖️

Consumer Care

Consumer counselling centres and legal awareness seminars for citizen protection.
