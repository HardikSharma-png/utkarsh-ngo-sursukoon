About Sur-Sukoon

Where every voice finds its moment. A celebration of music, culture, and community.

Our Identity

What is Sur-Sukoon?

Sur-Sukoon is a unique cultural initiative by Utkarsh Samajik Seva Sansthan — a platform designed to celebrate the rich musical heritage of India while giving everyday singers a professional, dignified stage.

The name itself speaks volumes — Sur (melody) and Sukoon (peace of mind). Sur-Sukoon is an evening where music transcends entertainment and becomes a form of soul nourishment. Whether you are a Bollywood aficionado, a ghazal lover, or a classical purist, this stage is yours.

"Ek Manch... Aapki Awaaz ke Naam" — One Stage, In the Name of Your Voice. A mantra that has guided every edition of Sur-Sukoon, ensuring that talent is celebrated regardless of background or profession.

Our Journey

History of Sur-Sukoon

2020

Sur-Sukoon — Season 1

The first edition of Sur-Sukoon brought together 15 performers and an audience of 200+ music lovers in Jaipur, marking the beginning of a beautiful tradition.

2021

Sur-Sukoon — Season 2

Expanding the reach, Season 2 featured performers from multiple cities and received coverage in local media.

2022

Sur-Sukoon — Season 3

A special theme evening dedicated to Rajasthani folk music and Bollywood classics that touched hundreds of hearts.

2023

Sur-Sukoon — Season 4

Our grandest edition yet, featuring 20 performers, live musicians, and an audience of 500+ at a prestigious venue in Jaipur.

2025

Sur-Sukoon — Season 5 (Upcoming)
